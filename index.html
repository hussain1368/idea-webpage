About the consortium

A clearer picture.
A stronger response.

Humanitarian challenges are connected.
Our evidence should be, too.

IDEA brings together the International Organization for Migration (IOM), REACH Initiative, the Centre for Operational Analysis and Research (COAR), and the International NGO Safety Organisation (INSO).

We connect assessment, research, analysis, independent monitoring, security and operational support in one coordinated evidence ecosystem. Together, we help partners understand needs, navigate complexity and turn insight into accountable action.

Our members

Different strengths.
Shared purpose.

Four organisations bring complementary perspectives to the questions that matter most to Myanmar's humanitarian response.

01 / IOM PMNT

Understanding people
and mobility

Granular, longitudinal evidence on displacement, migration, needs, socioeconomic conditions and recovery opportunities.

Assessments & capabilities

BMNA · Baseline Mobility and Needs Assessment: community-level population, mobility, needs and geographic conditions.

HCMS · Household Conditions and Mobility Survey: vulnerabilities, living conditions, mobility and socioeconomic trends.

RNAs · Rapid Needs Assessments: timely evidence on emerging crises and urgent needs.

02 / REACH

Making needs
and markets visible

Household and market evidence to inform humanitarian prioritisation, programme design and resource allocation.

Assessments & capabilities

MSNA · Multi-Sector Needs Assessment: household needs, severity and humanitarian prioritisation.

JMMI · Joint Market Monitoring Initiative: prices, availability, affordability and access to essential goods.

03 / COAR

Connecting context
and accountability

Independent monitoring and contextual analysis to understand delivery, results and the realities shaping response.

Services & capabilities

TPM · Third-Party Monitoring: independent verification and analysis of response implementation, delivery and results.

CASS · Context and Access Support Service: political, socioeconomic and operational insights for assessment planning and response decisions.

04 / INSO

Enabling safer
humanitarian action

Security and access insights, operational support and capacity building to help partners manage risk and operate safely.

Services & capabilities

Security and access analysis · Information on threats, operational constraints and changing security conditions.

Training and capacity building · Strengthening partners' ability to manage security risks and operate more safely.

IDEA connects evidence to decision-making at every level: strategic leadership and donors, national coordination, zonal and area-based mechanisms, humanitarian and development partners, civil society and affected communities.

Steering Committee +

Senior representatives of consortium members, OCHA and contributing donors provide strategic direction and oversight.

Expanded Steering Committee (SC+) +

A wider space for stakeholder consultation and feedback, keeping the consortium connected to the perspectives of the broader response.

Technical and Analysis Working Group +

The TAWG develops integrated information products, bringing collective evidence and analysis together to guide priorities and strategic and operational decisions.
